## 2. What the case says now

The first half (the `Reserve`-masked path, `RingTest.cpp:736-757`) is **not weakened**: same
`ASSERT_TRUE(Pop)`, same `EXPECT_EQ(flags & kRecPad, 0)`, same `EXPECT_NE(flags & kRecBorrowSlot, 0)`,
same `Invariants()`. Only the second half and one stale comment moved.

1. **`EXPECT_FALSE(Pop)` → `ASSERT_TRUE(Pop)`**, with a message that names the kind check as the
   defence: *"the stamped record vanished into Pop's wrap-filler skip. Pop's kind check — a filler
   must carry kind == kRingPadRecordKind as well as kRecPad — is the only thing standing between a
   header stamped with the CALL flags and every var-tail call being deleted from the stream with
   nothing logged on either side."*
2. `EXPECT_EQ(view.kind, MGPWireOp::DrawVbo)` — the kind is what Pop tells a record from a filler by.
3. The flags, stated exactly and by their translated names. `MGPipeCallFlagsFor(MGPWireOp::DrawVbo)`
   is `kHostSpan|kVarTail` (`PipeCalls.def:184`) = `0x0C`, and it arrives verbatim:
   * `EXPECT_EQ(view.flags, drawVboCallFlags)` — the header arrived as stamped;
   * `EXPECT_NE(flags & kRecPad, 0)` — bit 2, the `kVarTail` collision, **is still set**. The kind
     check narrows the *skip*; it does not scrub the bit. Naming the record is the decoder's job,
     and it can only do that because it got the record.
   * `EXPECT_NE(flags & kRecBorrowSlot, 0)` — bit 3, the `kHostSpan` collision, the lie about this
     record's lifetime (same wording as the first half already uses).
   * `EXPECT_EQ(flags & kRecVarTail, 0)` — bit 4. **Note for the integrator:** ID-43's text says the
     "lying `kRecBorrowSlot`/`kRecVarTail` bits" are visible. `kRecVarTail` is `kReplySlot`'s bit and
     `draw_vbo` is not a reply-slot call, so that bit is *clear* here, in the stamped half exactly as
     in the `Reserve` half. The case now pins it clear and says why, so `draw_vbo` growing
     `kReplySlot` is a red here rather than a silent change of what this case demonstrates.
   * `EXPECT_EQ(view.payloadSize, 16u)` and `EXPECT_TRUE(ring.Invariants())`.
4. **The other side of the control**, new: a header hand-stamped with `kRecPad` whose kind *is*
   `kRingPadRecordKind` is still skipped (`EXPECT_FALSE(Pop)`), `Invariants()` still holds. Without
   it, "the record is popped" could be satisfied by deleting the wrap-filler skip outright. Together
   the two halves say the whole ruling: **same bit, different kind, different fate.**
5. The stale prose at `RingTest.cpp:704-709` (the `static_assert` table's "the middle one is the
   worst: `Pop` treats `kRecPad` as a wrap filler and SKIPS the record") now reads in the
   conditional — that is what would happen *on the flag alone* — and points at the runtime half as
   the control. The `static_assert`s themselves are unchanged.
