## The M-2 cascade (why the milestone briefly regressed, and the two fixes it forced)

M-2 is correct and required, but it removed a crutch: with `liveHostBase()` reading the server shadow instead of
the client's `MappedData()`, the reduced-path VBO rendered a **shifted/empty** store (TriangleScenario's interior
came back blue, not green — `aColor` read one float early). Two real gaps that inproc-reading-`MappedData` had
masked, both now fixed:

1. **m-5's null was too broad** (`455b475c`): the generation-reset block runs on a twin's FIRST ensure
   (generation starts mismatched), and my first m-5 cut nulled a `hostBytes` a subdata had *just* staged in the
   same generation. `HasShadow` discriminates freed-on-context-loss from live-just-staged.
2. **the twin is lazy** (`c9d84e33`): `Ops_H_Create` is a no-op (D-A2), so a buffer's twin is minted at the
   first DRAW — *after* its `resource_subdata`. Under monolith that is fine (`MappedData` is the source); under
   split the subdata found no twin, returned early, and the bytes were lost. So under an active transport a
   subdata that finds no twin now MINTS one (`GetOrCreateBufferResourceForHandle`) to stage into. Monolith keeps
   the lazy twin.

Diagnosed by instrumentation, not assertion (`p5-v1-joint2-tri.log`: `Ops_H_SubData bytes=0x… (non-null)` then
`ensure hostBase=(nil) hasShadow=0`).
